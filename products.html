Commercial Kitchen Product Questions

Quick answers for Bangalore buyers comparing commercial kitchen equipment products.

Which commercial kitchen equipment products are available in Bangalore?

The catalogue includes gas ranges, dosa plates, idly steamers, SS work tables, sink units, trolleys, storage racks, chillers, Bain Marie counters, exhaust hoods, ducting, blowers and custom fabrication products.

Can I order multiple products for a complete restaurant kitchen?

Yes. Share the equipment list, kitchen size, quantity and delivery location to receive a practical quote for multiple commercial kitchen products.

Do you provide custom stainless steel sizes?

Yes. Custom dimensions can be discussed for stainless steel work tables, sinks, counters, shelves and layout-specific kitchen equipment.

Which SS 304 kitchen equipment products are commonly ordered?

Common SS 304 commercial kitchen equipment products include work tables, sink units, pickup counters, preparation counters, storage racks, trolleys, shelves and custom fabrication items for restaurants and hotels.

Can I get product guidance for hotel, restaurant or cloud kitchen setup?

Yes. Product guidance is available for hotel kitchens, restaurant kitchens, cafes, bakeries, canteens and cloud kitchens based on menu, kitchen size and expected service volume.
